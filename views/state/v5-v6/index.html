What makes it a report and not a confirmation:
- One source. The Hacker News, The Next Web and CyPro repeat Dream; CyPro's bulletin ends "Originally reported by securityweek.com". Three outlets, one origin. [[the-hacker-news-thehackernews-php2shell-roots-25-s, cypro-php2shell-exploit-roots-firewalls]]
- No victim, no vendor, no evidence published. Dream's artifacts are not public, and no statement from the firewall vendor, CISA, the US organisation or the PHP team about these victims is in the evidence.
- The press's own caveats. The Next Web: "TNW has not independently verified Dream's findings." CyPro: other sources do not independently verify Dream's victim details, and SecurityWeek reported two servers at the organisation where Dream says three, a difference it calls unresolved. - Craft CMS (21 October): "there is evidence that the vulnerabilities were being exploited even before the PHP updates were released". It does not say what the evidence is or whose, and names no victim. [[craft-cms-craft-cloud-and-php2shell]]
