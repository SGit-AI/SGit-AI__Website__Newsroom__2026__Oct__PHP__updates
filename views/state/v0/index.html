The line

Two critical flaws in the PHP engine were fixed on 21 October, after a disclosure that leaked two days early. The alarm was loud and the evidence of harm was thin: governments and others took sites offline as a precaution, a CDN counted exploit attempts in the millions, and no organisation had confirmed a compromise. PHP October 2026 (php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai) tracks PHP news for one month. This month that is one story, the two critical PHP security flaws, followed as evidence: every claim with a status and its sources, every source captured, dated and hashed into a private vault. Built and run by one editor and a team of software agents; the roles and the method are public, and the site is rebuilt from the vault, never written by hand.
