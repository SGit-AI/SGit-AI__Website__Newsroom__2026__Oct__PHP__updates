## What this version does not say, and why
The curator flagged six claims for checking against the ledger. This desk checked each against the saved page and the site's claims and contradiction records, and holds them back as worded:
- **That Japan's Prime Minister issued an emergency order.** One Vietnamese outlet says so. [[nguoiquansat-lo-hong-php2shell-de-doa-70-website-toan]] Reuters, CNN and USA Today, in the vault, describe Sanae Takaichi urging website operators, on X, to check whether their sites use PHP and to prepare to patch, and, in CNN's account, saying companies should consider suspending services if necessary: a request, not an order. [[rte-reuters-websites-go-dark, cnn-php2shell-websites-shut, usa-today-php2shell-live]]
- **That 22.6 million servers were put at risk.** TechTarget Japan gives the figure without its basis. [[techtarget-itmedia-tt-article-2610-23-2000002526]]
- **That exploitation is "confirmed" on WordPress, Drupal, Joomla, phpMyAdmin, Nextcloud and Magento.** Help Net Security does not say who confirmed it. [[helpnetsecurity-2026-10-22-php2shell-scanner-tool]] What the vault holds is researchers demonstrating code execution against these applications, which is not attackers succeeding against someone. [[calif-php2shell]]
- **That the bug was first spotted in the wild in 2024.** Mashable India gives no source. [[in-mashable-tech-115245-fbi-releases-php2shell-advisory-as-the-internet]] The earliest attempt in the record is Amazon's, on 17 October 2026. [[cyberpress-php2shell-exploits-predate-patches]] A 2024 audit by Quarkslab did find the out-of-memory issue without proving it exploitable, which may be where a 2024 date comes from. [[php-foundation-blog-security-release]]
- **That the flaws were identified on 20 October.** The Indian Express says so. [[indianexpress-article-technology-cert-in-php-vulnerability-alert-india-cyb]] Calif's discovery on 1 August and its report to PHP on 11 August are confirmed; 20 October is the day of the advance notice and the leak. [[calif-php2shell, php-foundation-blog-security-release]] SiliconANGLE's "August audit financed by OpenAI" is its own description of that August work, which the site records as found under OpenAI's initiative. [[siliconangle-2026-10-22-php2shell-attacks-began-four]]
- **How old the flaw is.** "14 years" (Clarin) and "since 2012" (Critic Arad) match Wiz's lower bound, PHP 5.4 and later, and are one estimate restated, not independent findings. [[clarin-us-news-php-security-vulnerabilities-how-ai-uncovered-massiv, criticarad-php2shell-cve-2026-109618-ghid-patch, wiz-blog-php2shell]] "Every version since PHP 5, from 2004" is watchTowr's account of the leaked report, as Ars Technica gives it. [[arstechnica-security-2026-10-php-maintainers-tell-web-devs-to-prepare-th]] The CVE records list only the supported branches. Which versions are affected is already an explained contradiction on [the fact-check desk](/factcheck/index.html); nobody in the vault names the commit that introduced the flaw.
