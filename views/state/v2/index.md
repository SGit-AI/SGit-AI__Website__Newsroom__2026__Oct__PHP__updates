## The line

Two and a half days after the release, php2shell still has no confirmed victim, and the story is now about what comes after the patch. The evidence vault holds 295 sources, 68 more than this desk's last version, most of them coverage from 21 and 22 October in a dozen languages, selected mechanically and read by the curator. They add detail and new countries; they do not add a victim.

## Compromise: none confirmed, and the question changes

No named organisation has confirmed a compromise through php2shell, at the vault's latest capture, 16:04 UTC on 23 October. The three named cases that were open in version 1 (ČVUT, GMO and DMM) are still open, and no new source changes any of them. [Lupa.cz](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/lupa-cvut-cyber-attack/) [Yahoo! News Japan](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/yahoo-jp-gmo-fx-login-leak/) [産経ニュース](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/sankei-dmm-service-halt/)

In Luxembourg, Paperjam reports that no financial institution, public service or operator has announced an intrusion, and that the authorities ask operators to search back for traces of compromise and to keep the evidence. [Paperjam](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/paperjam-article-php2shell-des-milliers-de-sites/) Singapore's Cyber Security Agency tells organisations to patch internet-facing systems first and to investigate whether they may already have been compromised. [CNA](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/channelnewsasia-singapore-php-vulnerabilities-csa-singapore-firms-cybersecur/) IT Social's headline on 23 October is the turn in one line: the update closes the flaw, but obliges a hunt for possible compromises; its body adds that the published figures measure attempts, not successful intrusions. [IT Social](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/itsocial-cybersecurite-cybersecurite-articles-php/)

That is the right next question, and it is a slower one. A patch is visible from outside; a break-in is found only by the operator who looks for it. The absence of a named victim is therefore weaker evidence each day that operators have not yet looked.

## Exploitation: attempts in millions, weaponisation unchanged

Cloudflare's count, as its chief executive put it, went from thousands of attempts in the ten hours before the announcement to millions by early on 22 October; attempts, not intrusions. [Matthew Prince, Cloudflare CEO on…](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/x-eastdakota-php2shell-stats/) [GinjFo](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/ginjfo-actualites-politique-et-economie-failles/) AhnLab, as reported, examined 100 GitHub repositories related to php2shell on 22 October and classed 27 as proof-of-concept exploits. [合同会社ロケットボーイズ](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/rocket-boys-security-measures-lab-php2shell-poc-expl/) This desk has not opened any of them, and will not.

One source contradicts another about Patchstack. Recorded Future reports that Patchstack received reports of "multiple active exploitation incidents" from hosting partners by 07:34 UTC on 21 October; PCMag quotes Patchstack saying that, shortly after the update, its telemetry showed "only probing attacks, with no hard evidence of breaches yet". [Recorded Future](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/recordedfuture-php2shell/) [PCMag](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/pcmag-news-hackers-already-trying-to-exploit-serious-php-flaw/) Both can be true at different times and of different things, reports received and telemetry seen, but neither names a victim.

The markers of weaponisation have not moved: neither CVE was in the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog at the old source watch's last check, 08:03 UTC on 23 October, and no Metasploit module had been merged. [CISA](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/cisa-kev-json/) This desk does not yet read the source watch's new vault, so that check is now sixteen hours old; the next version will say what it shows.

## Patching: the first national count of what is left

Risikomonitor examined nearly 690,000 servers linked to Switzerland on 22 October and found just over 23,000, about 3.5%, still affected; it says the real number may be higher because the PHP version cannot always be read. [ICTjournal](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/ictjournal-news-2026-10-22-deux-failles-critiques-d/) It is the first count in the vault of what remains after the fix in one country, and it sits beside VirusTotal's global picture, as reported, of about 758,000 end-of-life servers among the 1.1 million that disclose a version. [TugaTech](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/tugatech-t93092-milhoes-de-servidores-php-expostos-ao-risco/) The two do not measure the same thing: one counts servers still affected in one country, the other counts versions anywhere.

The NCSC-NL inventory has not changed since 12:50 UTC on 23 October: 109 of 751 products fixed, 389 under investigation. [NCSC-NL](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/ncsc-nl-php2shell-software-list-letters/)

## The response spreads

More national authorities are in the record. Peru's Centro Nacional de Seguridad Digital called php2shell a critical threat in its report of 21 October. [Agencia Andina](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/andina-agencia-noticia-peru-y-otros-paises-advi/) Morocco's DGSSI issued a critical bulletin the same day. [Morocco World News](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/moroccoworldnews-2026-10-343335-moroccos-dgssi-warns-of-critical-php2shell-vu/) Korea's science ministry raised the private-sector cyber crisis alert from "interest" to "caution", as reported by Shin-A Ilbo. [신아일보](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/shinailbo-news-articleview-html/) In the Netherlands, MPs have put questions to the justice and security minister about taking government sites offline. [Security.NL](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/security-nl-kamervragen/) Wiz warns that the flaws can also let an attacker read process memory holding passwords, tokens, API keys and certificates: a patched server that was reachable before may still have leaked secrets that need changing. [Wiz](https://refs.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/sources/wiz-blog-php2shell/)

## What would change this state

- A named organisation confirming a compromise, which is now likelier to come from an operator's own search than from a headline.

- A KEV listing, or a merged Metasploit module.

- More national counts like Switzerland's, so the size of what is left can be compared country by country.

- The 389 inventory rows under investigation resolving, and fixes for the security appliances.
