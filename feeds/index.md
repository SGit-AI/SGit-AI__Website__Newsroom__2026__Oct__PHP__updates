# Feeds: what is new and what changed, wherever you read

Five RSS feeds: one for everything, one for each part of the site. An item enters a feed when it is new, and again every time what it says changes, so a fact that moves from reported to confirmed reaches you as an entry of its own, with a link to what changed. The release log has an Atom feed of its own, [feed.xml](https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/feed.xml).

### Everything `feeds/all.xml`

Every new or changed fact, page, briefing, story, question, source and dated event.

[The feed](../feeds/all.xml) · [read it here, with what you have read marked](https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/reader/#site-all) · or copy `https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/feeds/all.xml` into any feed reader
