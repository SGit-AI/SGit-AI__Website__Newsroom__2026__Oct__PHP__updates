## The line

Two critical flaws in the PHP engine were fixed on 21 October, after a disclosure that leaked two days early. The alarm was loud and the evidence of harm was thin: governments and others took sites offline as a precaution, a CDN counted exploit attempts in the millions, and no organisation had confirmed a compromise.
