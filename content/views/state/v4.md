## The line

php2shell is now officially exploited, and still has no named victim. One of the two lead measures moved: CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog went from neither CVE to both. The other did not: no organisation has said it was compromised through these flaws. This version changes only what follows from the listing; every other measure is carried from version 3 with its own as-of time.

## Exploitation: on CISA's list

CISA's alert of 26 October says it "has added two new vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) Catalog": CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. The catalog's version 2026.10.26, released at 22:24 UTC on 26 October and captured by the newsroom at 00:45 UTC on 27 October, lists both with the date added 26 October, a due date of 29 October and known ransomware use "Unknown". [[cisa-alert-kev-20261026, cisa-kev-json, cisa-kev-catalog]]

What it establishes: CISA holds evidence that the flaws are exploited. The alert says an addition "must have a CVE ID, evidence of exploitation, and clear mitigation guidance". [[cisa-alert-kev-20261026]] It supersedes CISA's alert of 21 October, which said it "has not observed exploitation of these vulnerabilities as of the time of publication", so the US leaves the count of authorities that know of no successful exploitation, which falls from six to five. [[cisa-alert-php-20261021]] The due date binds only US federal civilian agencies under BOD 26-04; the alert encourages every organisation to prioritise the catalog. "Unknown" in the ransomware field is the catalog's value, neither a finding of ransomware use nor a finding that there is none. [[cisa-alert-kev-20261026, cisa-kev-json]]

The last PHP core flaw, CVE-2024-4577, was listed three days after disclosure; this one was listed five days after the fixes of 21 October. [[akamai-blog-cve-2024-4577-one-day, cisa-kev-json]]

## Compromise: still none named

What the listing does not establish: a victim. The alert and the two catalog entries name no compromised organisation, no threat actor and no ransomware campaign. [[cisa-alert-kev-20261026, cisa-kev-catalog]] No named organisation has said it was compromised through php2shell at the vault's latest capture, 00:45 UTC on 27 October, across 562 sources. Exploited is CISA's finding about the flaws; compromised would need an organisation saying so, and none has. Sophos's withdrawn claim and the sectors that report nothing, in version 3, stand. [[cybersecuritynews-critical-php2shell-exploit-code, americanbanker-news-banks-dodge-early-damage-from-php2s]]

CISA says it also updated its PHP alert of 21 October. The newsroom's capture of that alert predates the update, so what the new text says is not yet in the evidence.

## What would change this state

- A named organisation confirming a compromise, most likely from its own search.
- CISA, or anyone, naming a victim, or the updated PHP alert saying what evidence CISA holds.
- A merged Metasploit module.
- The NCSC-NL inventory moving again.
