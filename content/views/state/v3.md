## The line

php2shell is three days old and still has no confirmed victim. What moved since version 2 is the other side of the ledger: a claim of success that did not survive, and more sectors saying, in public, that nothing has happened to them yet. The evidence vault holds 328 sources, 33 more than version 2, from the curator's two wakes this morning.

## Compromise: none confirmed, and one claim withdrawn

No named organisation has said it was compromised through php2shell, at the vault's latest capture, 08:37 UTC on 24 October. The three named cases that were open (ČVUT, GMO and DMM) are still open; no new source changes them. [[lupa-cvut-cyber-attack, yahoo-jp-gmo-fx-login-leak, sankei-dmm-service-halt]]

The first claim of a successful compromise by a security vendor has been withdrawn. Sophos, in a correction issued at 21:21 UTC on 22 October and reported by Cyber Security News, said its customer data showed scanning and a small number of failed exploit attempts and no evidence of a successful compromise. One attempt tried to deliver Potassium, a likely Mirai botnet variant; it was blocked. [[cybersecuritynews-critical-php2shell-exploit-code]] Patchstack's two accounts, reports of incidents from hosting partners and telemetry showing only probing, are still not reconciled. [[recordedfuture-php2shell, pcmag-news-hackers-already-trying-to-exploit-serious-php-flaw]]

Sectors are now saying so in public. American Banker: "no U.S. bank or credit union has publicly reported a compromise". [[americanbanker-news-banks-dodge-early-damage-from-php2s]] BTC-ECHO: no evidence that large crypto exchanges or payment providers were compromised. [[btc-echo-news-krypto-boersen-akut-bedroht-kritisc]] Officials in Germany, the Netherlands and Japan say they have not seen an attempt succeed. [[the-next-web-php2shell-no-confirmed-breaches]] Each is an absence of reports at a point in time, not a finding that nothing happened, and the advice of version 2 stands: the answer will come from operators searching their own systems. [[paperjam-article-php2shell-des-milliers-de-sites]]

## Exploitation: public code, no weaponisation markers

Public proof-of-concept code is available, and attackers have tried to use it to build botnets; Sophos says its own researchers found working exploitation difficult, even with AI support. [[cybersecuritynews-critical-php2shell-exploit-code]] Neither CVE was in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, and no Metasploit module had been merged, at the Pulse vault's check at 05:53 UTC today, the freshest check in any vault this desk reads. [[cisa-kev-json]] The last PHP core flaw was listed within three days; today is the third day after this one. [[akamai-blog-cve-2024-4577-one-day]]

## Exposure and patching

The 22.6 million figure that circulated without a basis is Palo Alto Networks' count of exposed instances potentially vulnerable, made on 21 October, now attributed in two captured reports; it counts exposure, not compromise. [[techtarget-cybersecurity-news-366652444-php2shell-2, insurancebusiness-uk-councils-met]] The NCSC-NL inventory has not changed since 12:50 UTC yesterday: 109 of 751 products fixed, 389 under investigation. [[ncsc-nl-php2shell-software-list-letters]]

## Offline

More organisations that took sites down are in the record: in Switzerland, the EPFL, Basel-Stadt's administration, the University of Geneva and the insurer La Mobilière. [[24heures-php2shell-qui-est-affecte-par-la-faille]] All of them describe precautions.

## What this version changed on the site

The no-compromise claim and its evidence page are as of 08:37 UTC on 24 October; the 22 new claims from the vault are reviewed, 14 noted or agreeing, 6 used and 2 held back (a Check Point figure whose "impacted" is undefined, and a Chinese summary's account of which model Calif used); and the verdict on the 22.6 million figure is revised in the open on [the claims waiting for review](/claims/review/index.html).

## What would change this state

- A named organisation confirming a compromise, most likely from its own search.
- A KEV listing, or a merged Metasploit module.
- Patchstack reconciling its two accounts.
- The NCSC-NL inventory moving again.
