From about 15:00 UTC on 20 October the CVE IDs were loose with a wrong score; by 19:30 the project had pre-announced a release a day early; by 20:00 one of the reporters had published a demonstration video; by 23:00 mitigation advice was going to critical-infrastructure providers; by 02:00 on the 21st Amazon was seeing proof-of-concept attempts; by 03:02 Cloudflare's rules were live; by 07:41 Chainguard had shipped patched images; by 08:36 a sensor network had its first hit. The release went out at 13:34 UTC.
