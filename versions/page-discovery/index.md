# Who found php2shell, and what the AI did Calif, OpenAI and Wiz each claim the discovery of php2shell; their own timelines side by side, the 2024 audit that saw it first, the credits on the advisories, and what the sources do and do not say about the models. # Who found it, and how Three research parties say they found php2shell, and all three say AI helped. Their own timelines, placed side by side, do not conflict on dates: Calif first (1 to 11 August), OpenAI the same day as Calif's report, Wiz independently eight days later. They conflict on framing: Wiz's *Wiz Research uncovers*, OpenAI's *frontier AI was able to find this vulnerability ahead of attackers*, and a Community Note on OpenAI's post pointing at Calif's August hash. ### Calif - 2026-07-30 audit begins under Patch the Planet - 2026-08-01 multiple paths to RCE found - 2026-08-05 hash commitment posted on X - 2026-08-11 first bug reported to PHP - 2026-08-14 RCE PoC shared; confirmed 08-15 - 2026-08-21 second bug reported Calif Ryan Austin , Calif on X ### OpenAI - 2026-08-11 same root cause reported, same day as Calif (per the Foundation) - 2026-09-18 further attack vectors - coalition of maintainers, researchers and providers over two months - credit: amyb-oai on both advisories PHP Foundation OpenAI on X PHP ### Wiz - 2026-08-19 first vulnerability found (Wiz Atlas, Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-5.6 Sol) - 2026-08-20 RCE confirmed on Drupal - 2026-08-21 RCE confirmed on WordPress - 2026-08-24 reported to PHP - 2026-09-21 PHP acknowledges - credit: sagitzadik-wiz Wiz ### Before them - 2024 Quarkslab audit (OSTIF, Sovereign Tech Agency) surfaces the OOM issue - 2024 a Foundation-contracted developer finds first signs; exploit theorised, no vector - credit: tomash-areion on both advisories, named nowhere else PHP Foundation Calif PHP ## What the AI did The honest answer from the captured sources is: not enough is said. Calif says *No special harness, or access to models that weren't generally available to everyone were used in the initial bug discovery* and that the researcher *asked my LLM companion for help*; it names no model. Wiz names three tools and quotes Google DeepMind's head of Gemini security: *a single researcher completed in a week what previously would have taken a world-class team months of work.* OpenAI's blog, which reportedly names GPT-5.6 Sol, is behind a bot challenge from this capture environment. The PHP Foundation adds that the project was separately scanned with Anthropic's Claude Mythos through Alpha-Omega and that OpenAI's Daybreak Red validated reports and exploits. What is established is the shape: a bug class seen in 2024 and left because nobody could prove exploitation, then three groups with model assistance finding working exploits within three weeks of each other in August 2026. The BBC's quote, *a complex chain of steps, at the edge of what human researchers could practically achieve*, is not on any captured researcher page.
