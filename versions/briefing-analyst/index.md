Briefing · Analyst or advisor # How did coordinated disclosure fail, and what does AI-assisted discovery change? What this incident says about coordinated disclosure once models can find bugs, and once a leak can become a proof of concept faster than a patch can ship. The claims below are the evidence; the reading is the site's. ## What to decide or do - The window between leak and release was 22.5 hours and the window between first honeypot hit and release was four days. Both are shorter than the two months the fix took. - Three AI-assisted teams converged on a bug class seen and shelved in 2024. The constraint that failed in 2024 was proving exploitability, and that is what models now appear to lower the cost of. - The defensive side moved early too: WAF rules 9 hours ahead, images 6 hours ahead (against the embargo), agencies within the hour. The system is faster in both directions. - The public numbers were not produced for decision-making and should not be used for it; the one agency that counted got about 6,000 in a country of 1.9 million people. - Agencies diverged on the hardest question, offline or not, along lines that look institutional rather than technical. ## The facts this rests on - confirmed two or more primary sources agree, or the fact was counted from a captured file - reported one party says so, and it has not been contradicted - disputed the sources disagree, and both readings are shown - contradicted a primary source says otherwise - unverified even the reporting outlet could not check it | Claim | Who says it | Status | Sources | |---|---|---|---| | Calif found the flaws first: discovery 1 August, hash commitment 5 August, report to PHP 11 AugustThe hash tweet is dated 5 August 2026 on X; its pre-image has not been published, so what it commits to cannot be checked. | Calif; PHP Foundation (report date) | confirmed | Calif Ryan Austin , Calif on X PHP Foundation | | The GitHub advisories credit four reporters: lcfr-hax (Calif), sagitzadik-wiz (Wiz), amyb-oai (OpenAI) and tomash-areiontomash-areion is not named in the PHP Foundation's thanks and appears nowhere else on this beat. | GitHub security advisories | confirmed | PHP PHP Daubois | | The out-of-memory issue was found in a 2024 audit by Quarkslab (funded through OSTIF and the Sovereign Tech Agency) but exploitation was not proven, so it was not fixedTwo parties agree.
