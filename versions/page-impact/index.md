The one operator who measured it served 94% of its traffic from cache for 13 hours. - **Attempts are not compromises.** Cloudflare's millions and Amazon's honeypot hits are requests matching a shape at the edge or against a sensor. Nobody has published a count of successful exploitations, and the parties best placed to see one (CISA, Huntress, Imperva) report none. - **Absence of evidence is weak here.** The detection authors are explicit that default web logs cannot show the attack shape and that no standard log carries the worker id the cross-request bug needs.
