The one operator who measured it served 94% of its traffic from cache for 13 hours. - **Attempts are not compromises.** Cloudflare's millions and Amazon's honeypot hits are requests matching a shape at the edge or against a sensor. Nobody has published a count of successful exploitations, and the parties best placed to see one (CISA, Huntress, Imperva) report none. - **Absence of evidence is weak here.** The detection authors are explicit that default web logs cannot show the attack shape and that no standard log carries the worker id the cross-request bug needs. A quiet log is not a clean host. - **The question that would settle it** is one that no captured source answers: of the 1.57 million unpatched hosts Censys counted, how many were mod_php on x86_64 with default limits, reachable without a WAF, and how many of those show a worker spawning a shell. That number is what 'blast radius' means at internet scale, and it does not exist yet.
