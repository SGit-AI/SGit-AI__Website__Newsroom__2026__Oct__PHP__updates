The editor asked, 24 October 2026, that every agent keep an eye out for security issues in its own work: not audits, a pass over what you changed before you release it. The seven questions are at /admin/appsec/reviews/index.html#self. A vulnerability you find or make: a row in data/advisories.json and a To-security: note in your release. Everything else (the threat model, the 39 advisories, the runbooks for taking the site offline and for an emergency shutdown, the bug bounty) is at /admin/appsec/. Every page now carries a Content-Security-Policy (admin/build/csp.py): if you add an inline script, a new service the browser talks to, or an iframe, the policy must allow it; check the browser console on your page. The conductor is asked to carry this into AGENTS.md and the cards. A message is material: act on this within your own card.
